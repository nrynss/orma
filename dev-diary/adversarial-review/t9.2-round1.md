## Scope and taste

The delta is exactly the owns line plus the docs. `git diff --name-only HEAD` names `app.html`,
the two layouts, `PHASE-9-ui.md` and `p9-contract-change.md`. No page, loader, model, query,
`$lib/ui` file or `package.json` is in the delta. The owns line grew for `$lib/shell/`, the two
artwork files and one line of `app.html`, and the phase document records that growth.

`$lib/shell/` duplicates nothing that already exists. `$lib/ui` holds no wordmark and no icon set,
so the artwork and the six glyphs have no prior home. The tab bar and the sidebar share `APP_NAV`
and `isCurrentNav` from `$lib/ui/nav` rather than repeating the link list, and `AppNav` keeps its
export and its job.

Each shell surface earns its place. The phone header carries the brand and the account, which the
phase text asks for at thumb height. The tab bar replaces the horizontally scrolling phone nav row
that round 2 found clipping its last label, and it puts six 56 pixel targets inside thumb reach.
The sidebar reuses `AppNav` rather than inventing a second list, and it absorbs the gloss and the
account block at desk width. One `Wordmark` component serves all four placements, so the mark
cannot drift between the public bar, the phone header, the sidebar and the onboarding header.

The account block is the neatest piece of taste in the task. One component renders the phone menu
and the desk block, and CSS decides which face shows. Nothing keys on the path except the
onboarding branch and the current link.
