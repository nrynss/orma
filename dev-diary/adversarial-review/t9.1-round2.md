## The side effect the remediation recorded, re-measured

The remediation flagged one consequence of the global reset and asked for it to be checked. The
`.o-switch-track` rule declares 46 by 28 with 3 pixels of padding and a 22 pixel thumb.

I measured the track twice, once as it stands and once with `box-sizing: content-box` forced back
on that one rule.

| State | Track | Thumb inset, off | Thumb inset, on | Vertical inset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current tree, with the reset | 46 by 28 | 3 left, 21 right | 21 left, 3 right | 3 top, 3 bottom |
| `content-box` forced on the track | 52 by 34 | 3 left, 27 right | 21 left, 9 right | 6 top, 6 bottom |

The reset does not shrink the control against its own declaration. It makes the thumb flush and
symmetric, which is what the rule always described. Without the reset the thumb stops 9 pixels
short of the track's right end and floats 6 pixels from the top and bottom instead of 3. I judge
the side effect a fix rather than a regression, and I keep it.

Every interactive target still measures 44 pixels tall in the gallery, over the button, the switch
row, the field control and the nav link.
