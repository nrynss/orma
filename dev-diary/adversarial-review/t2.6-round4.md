earlier capture-only receiver that no longer exists in the tree; none of their
findings is carried into this round (see "Zero residue" below). The receiver
reviewed here checks the path secret, validates the envelope against the
`call-e-event-id` header, inserts the event id into `webhook_events`,
conditionally claims it, re-fetches `GET /v1/calls/{data.id}` with the bearer
key, matches the re-fetch to an Orma `call_run`, and records state. The body is
never a fact: the only writes are to `webhook_events`.

The external delivery blocker recorded in
`dev-diary/adversarial-review/t2.6-contract-change.md` is not raised as a
finding here.

| M | `supabase/functions/calle-webhook/index.ts:152-161`, `:226-232` | A row left in `refreshing` is never reclaimable.
