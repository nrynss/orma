# T2.1 contract change

Round one found that the original seam could not satisfy the required nightly
schedule. The task now owns a dedicated migration for its `pg_cron` job.

| Where | What | Pin | Mutation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dev-diary/PHASE-2-call-engine.md` T2.1 ownership | Add `supabase/migrations/20260912150000_schedule_materialise.sql` to the task seam. | Round-one C1 showed no tracked `cron.schedule` call for materialise. | Removing the migration leaves production materialisation dependent on a manual request. |
| `dev-diary/PHASE-2-call-engine.md` T2.1 ownership | Add the function configuration and secret-inventory files required to authorize a Vault-backed scheduler request. | Round-two C1 showed that a publishable key cannot authenticate the scheduler to a service-role-writing function. | Removing the handler authorization or failing to inventory its secret makes the scheduler unreachable or exposes the endpoint. |
