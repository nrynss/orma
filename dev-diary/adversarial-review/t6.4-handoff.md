# T6.4 handoff: Patterns

Implementation role. This work is ready for independent review.

## What exists now

`/app/patterns` is a browser-rendered owner view under RLS. It reads the signed-in
profile zone, that owner's latest `pattern_reports` row, that owner's `call_runs`
rows, and that owner's `kind = pattern` deliveries. It never touches another
account, another route, `web/src/lib`, or `web/package.json`.
- The loader (`+page.ts`, `ssr = false`, `prerender = false`, mirroring the items loader)
  fetches the latest report. It also fetches all of the owner's runs in chronological
  order and all of the owner's pattern deliveries.
- The report renders the period label from the facts window and the prose as plain
  paragraphs. It renders a Facts table with every facts key beside its value. The table
  is headed Computed by SQL with the design caption.
- The mood trend is an inline SVG drawn from the owner's `call_runs.mood` rows
  whose `local_date` falls inside the report period, in chronological order.
  Known moods sit on their lane. Unknown or missing moods and unanswered runs
  render hollow on the lower lane, under the design legend.
- The not-yet panel renders when no report exists or when
  `facts.sufficient_history` is not true. It shows completed runs counted from
  rows and the required days from the facts value, falling back to 3. It never
  extrapolates.
- The sent line renders only when a `kind = pattern` delivery with `sent_at`
  carries a null `call_run_id` or one inside the report period. The date renders
  in the profile timezone.

The harness fixture in `scripts/p6-local-stack.sh` (`insert_harness_rows` only)
adds three completed morning runs for the seed account. Their moods are ok, low and
stressed. It adds one `pattern_reports` row whose facts come from the real
`compute_pattern_facts` SQL over the last seven complete local days. It also adds one
telegram pattern delivery sent a day ago.
