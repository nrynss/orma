## Developer-environment note, not a finding

The session cookie carries `Secure`, so browsers reject it on plain http
origins other than `localhost`. Sign-in against `localhost:5174` works
(adb reverse, or a local browser); sign-in against
`http://192.168.1.4:5174` from another device or a LAN-addressed desktop
cannot hold the session. This predates P9, ships no risk to production
(HTTPS), and needs no code change. Harness reviewers should use the cookie
injection recipe in the handoff log or `adb reverse`.
