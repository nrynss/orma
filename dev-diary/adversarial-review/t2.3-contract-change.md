# T2.3 contract change

The original file-only seam could not provide atomic PostgreSQL claims or the
required minute scheduler. T2.3 now owns its dedicated RPC and `pg_cron`
migration.

| Where | What | Pin | Mutation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dev-diary/PHASE-2-call-engine.md` T2.3 ownership | Add `supabase/migrations/20260912161000_tick.sql`. | PostgREST cannot issue `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, and no claim RPC or tick job exists. | Removing the RPC permits overlapping ticks to claim one run or makes claims impossible. |
| `dev-diary/PHASE-2-call-engine.md` T2.3 ownership | Add `supabase/migrations/test-tick-claims.sh` for the isolated two-session claim pin. | Round-one H1 rejected a mocked concurrency proof. | Removing the runner leaves `SKIP LOCKED` unproven against PostgreSQL. |
