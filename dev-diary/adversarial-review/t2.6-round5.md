## What I could not pin

I could not pin the four pure gate checks. The missing-header 400, the oversized
body 413, the non-terminal-type 400 and the non-POST 405 each reject before any
dependency runs. Removing the terminal-type check, the body-size gate or the
method gate still passes all 11 tests, as the coverage table shows. The fixtures
and mocks only use small POST bodies with terminal types, so no in-file check
observes these gates. Round 4 saw them only through an external probe.

I could not pin a residual state-machine gap to a functional loss.
`supabase/functions/calle-webhook/index.ts:18-19`. A row that enters `refreshing`
leaves it only through this invocation, through the error release, or through a
later delivery that arrives after the 5-minute lease. If the invocation is killed
and the provider stops retrying inside the lease, the row stays `refreshing` and
that delivery is answered 200 without processing. No sweeper touches
`webhook_events`. This residue is pre-existing, and polling reconciles the call,
so I do not raise it as a finding. I could not prove how long CALL-E retries,
because that is external.

I could not pin a leak from the recorded fixture body. The body carries masked
phone numbers and transcript text, and the probe shows none of it reaches
`call_events`. The receiver never logs the body. That matches the contract.
